Quick command flyouts

The eight strip entries whose flyout today is a copied context submenu. Each artboard shows today's submenu as the reference, then two designed options, A and B, in the style of the Cleared for takeoff and pattern-entry flyouts: glyph rows that each show the command they send, and inline boxes where a value is typed. A and B differ in one real open choice, named in each option's title. Under every option: how it works, and its worst case in use.

Click any row or chip: the line under the lane says what it sends. Data is illustrative KOAK traffic and taxi routes (taxiway geometry not checked against the layout); the approach ids are KOAK's from the bundled CIFP. The menus follow the settled YAAT-545 ruling, so the strip has no label row.

Open choices this canvas does not draw

Each needs the owner's word or a mock of its own before a brief.

  1. Live lists while open. Follow, Give way to, Hold short of and Exit left/right are built when the menu opens. Whether a designed flyout refills while open (as Push back to does through FollowInFlyout), and how rows that move or vanish behave under the pointer, is not drawn.
  2. Keyboard paths. Arrow keys across chips and tabs, where focus lands on open (the first row, or a filter or typed box), and what Enter does in each box: not drawn beyond the Follow filter.
  3. Route preview and aircraft highlight on chips and tabs. Today a row previews its route (taxi, hold short) or highlights its aircraft (Follow, Give way) on hover; whether a chip, a tab or a ladder stop does the same is open.
  4. Lazy search states. Taxi to runway's "Finding runway entries…" and Other runways' later search: only the placeholder is shown; how a runway chip or tab looks while its search runs, and its "No runway entry found" state, are not drawn.
  5. Exit glyph turned to the exit's angle. The exits-ahead list carries taxiway, side, distance and planned, but no exit angle, so a glyph turned per exit (high-speed versus 90°) has no data behind it.
  6. Screen edge and the 480 px cap. A flyout opens on the menu's left near the screen's right edge; how the chip rows, the runway diagram and the tab bars wrap at the 480 px menu cap and on a short screen is not drawn.
  7. Point menu strip and the popup pickers. Ruled into YAAT-569's scope (Taxi here, Taxi to runway per end, Push to; Fly heading, Maintain altitude, Assign speed, Direct to and the rest), but not in this batch.
  8. Order of the work. Which design is built first is not a screen choice.
Row templates reproduced from MenuCommandRow.cs, MenuGlyphRow.cs and TakeoffFlyout.cs: Inter text, commands in JetBrains Mono at 70%, outlined badges, 22 px glyphs at stroke 1.9 coloured by family, the Fluent dark menu surface, strip cells 40 px with the corner notch.
